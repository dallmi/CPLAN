Executive read-out
What is the most expensive gap, and what does it cost?
The report read 2’014 rows to build this board. 431 of the activities in scope carry no audience band, which is the gap that reaches furthest: an activity with no band sits outside every audience cut, so any statement about who the plan addresses is made without it. The executive fields are the other side of the same problem — they are recorded on a minority of the plan, so the leadership picture is a floor and never a total. Neither is a planning failure; both are fields nobody was asked to fill. Chase the audience band first: it is one field, it is on every form, and it unlocks the cut that gets asked for most.
